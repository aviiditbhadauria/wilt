// progressive friction

Three tiers. One gentle intervention.

Wilt tracks active time per domain and persists it daily. As your time crosses each threshold, the friction deepens.

LEVEL 1 · MILD

Black & white + lag

The feed desaturates to black & white, cuts to a full black screen in brief interruptions, and every interaction — liking a post, tapping a comment — is delayed so the feed feels sluggish and unresponsive.

grayscale(100%)
+ blackout() + delayInteraction()
LEVEL 2 · MEDIUM

3G loading + jumbled words

Builds on mild: interactions load as if on a slow 3G connection, and the text gets its letters shuffled so reading becomes effortful. Images stay perfectly sharp — they are never blurred at this level.

+ throttle(3G)
+ scrambleText() · no blur
LEVEL 3 · HEAVY

Blur & silent barrier

Images and video blur out, and the page becomes completely unresponsive — a digital barrier with no visible prompt. No dialog, overlay, or warning is shown to the user.

+ blur(16px)
+ blockAllEvents() · invisible

Made to fit your habits

Wilt ships with sensible defaults, but the whole point is that you stay in control. Tune it until the nudge feels right.

Custom time limits

Your thresholds, your rules

Set how long is "too long" before each friction tier kicks in. Defaults are 1 / 3 / 5 minutes, but you can stretch them to 120 minutes or tighten them to a single minute — as long as they stay ascending.

Custom websites

Add any domain

Six sites are tracked out of the box, but the real power is yours. Type any domain into the popup or side panel to start applying friction there too — news sites, forums, your own weak spots.

Your control center

Popup & side panel

Toggle Wilt on or off in a click, watch live per-site timers, manage sites and thresholds from a tidy popup — or keep a side panel open while you work.

Private by design

Stays on your machine

All time-tracking lives in your browser's local storage. Wilt never reads what you type or sends your data anywhere — it only changes how the page looks.

Smart by default

SPA & daily aware

Wilt understands single-page apps, so switching feeds inside one site still counts. Timers reset every day at midnight so each session starts fresh.

Calm intervention

Escalates gently

No alarms, no shame. Just gradual visual friction — black-screen interruptions, harder-to-read words, blurred media — until closing the tab feels like the relief it is.
